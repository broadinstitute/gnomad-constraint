rr follow-up: why more DNMs did worse, and the outlier/dispersion choice

2026-10-27 · gnomAD v4.1.1 per-variant expected · 18,406 Ensembl MANE Select transcripts · follows rr_syn_dnm_report.html

What has been done

  • rr is in the pipeline: Gnocchi's regional mutation-rate model retrained on synonymous DNMs, applied per variant after calibration, normalized per context to the exome; it reproduces legacy syn_adj_r exactly, and unadjusted values are kept.
  • Settled options: normalization on; clipping removed (no effect); chrX/Y features built, masking off (it hurts); non-coding adj_r (PR #55) rejected (about 4× too strong).
  • Effect: synonymous outliers 270 → about 220 and core-set dispersion 2.91 → about 2.5.
  • Satterstrom 2026 DNMs (17,059 → 23,336, extending the old DDD+ASD set) did worse at the Bonferroni cutoff at full strength, with the loss in non-CpG contexts.
  • Every rr version is too strong; fitting the strength (rrα) together with a looser cutoff gives the lowest dispersion. The odds-ratio rr scale and separate CpG/non-CpG strengths did not help.
  • This report: three tests of why more DNMs did worse, and a search for the option with the fewest synonymous outliers and the lowest dispersion.

Methods in brief

  • Metrics: outliers = transcripts with |z| > 8 (all 18,406); dispersion = level-free mean z² on the core set (17,032 unflagged transcripts, expected ≥ 20; this leaves out the unadjusted synonymous outliers, see Checks). Out-of-fold = chromosomes split into odd and even halves (chrX odd, chrY even), each half scored with models trained on the other.
  • Scoring: one Dataproc export of synonymous expected counts per transcript × 1 kb window × context; rr is then applied locally with the pipeline's rules. It matches the pipeline to 1e-14 per transcript and the Dataproc results to six decimals. Local retraining uses the pipeline's code and selects identical features.

Conclusion of tests 1–3

Bonferroni selection on DNMs is a noisy filter for features that matter in gnomAD. More DNMs changed which features pass, adding weaker ones and losing some good ones, and at full strength that choice dominates. The DNMs themselves are not the problem.

Fewest outliers and lowest dispersion

840 configurations were scored: 14 model sets (old and new DNMs at five cutoffs, new DNMs with the old features, and new-DNM sets without CpG_island) × α 0.5–1.5, one strength or separate CpG/non-CpG strengths, out-of-fold and with all-chromosome models. To avoid flattering the winner of many noisy counts, options were also chosen on odd-chromosome transcripts and scored on even ones, and the reverse; outlier differences have 95% intervals from 2,000 bootstrap resamples.

Checks on the metrics

The core set leaves out the unadjusted synonymous outliers. “Unflagged” means no v4.1.2 constraint flags, and all 270 transcripts with |z| > 8 under the unadjusted expected carry outlier_syn. That selects on the outcome and favors weaker rr. Core-set dispersion (also the slope set) is affected; outlier counts, over all transcripts, are not. Recomputed without that selection:

  • Out-of-fold is not flattered by feature selection. Out-of-fold models hold out one chromosome half, but features were selected on all chromosomes. Redoing selection within each half: new 1e-2 best dispersion 2.482 → 2.492, outliers at α 0.625 222 → 224; new Bonferroni 2.526 → 2.526.
  • Design caveat (from legacy): the training background excludes sites seen in gnomAD v4.1 exomes, the data rr is scored on. Regions where gnomAD sees more variants get a thinner background and so a higher rr, which may be part of why every rr is too strong. It affects all options alike; testing it needs a training set rebuilt without that exclusion.

Recommendation

  • Use the new Satterstrom 2026 DNMs, the 1e-2 cutoff, and one strength α = 0.625, normalized per context, chrX/Y unmasked. α 0.625 is also the best α on core sets not selected on synonymous counts.
  • To lean further toward outliers: α = 0.75 (2 fewer out-of-fold, not significant; dispersion about equal to the reference: −0.023 out-of-fold, +0.011 with all-chromosome models).
  • Next: add the strength setting to --compute-rr, then run the constraint pipeline once. For later evaluations, define the core set without the outcome flags (remove only no-expected and low mapping-quality/coverage genes).
